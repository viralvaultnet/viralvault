Video & Image Downloader

Open direct video and image links, preview supported media, and save files when your browser and the source website allow it. Simple, free, and mobile-friendly.

Use a direct link to a media file (such as .mp4, .webm, .jpg, .png, or .webp), not a video-page URL. Download availability depends on the source server and browser.

How to use ViralVault

Save media you own or have permission to download. ViralVault runs in your browser and does not bypass private accounts, paywalls, DRM, or platform restrictions.

1. Copy a direct link

Copy the direct URL of a video or image file from a source that permits downloading.

2. Paste and preview

Choose Video link or Image link, paste the URL, then press Open media to check if it can be displayed.

3. Save your file

Press Download file. Some websites block browser downloads; if that happens, use the source website's official download option.

About ViralVault

ViralVault is a simple online utility for working with direct video and image file links. The goal is to offer a clear, mobile-friendly experience without requiring an account. Results depend on the URL, browser, and permissions set by the source website.

Frequently asked questions

Can ViralVault download any video from any website?

No. This tool supports direct media-file links. A link to a page on a social platform is not usually a direct video file. Use the platform's official download feature and follow its terms.

Why does the download button sometimes not work?

Some source websites block cross-origin downloads or require a login. Your browser may open the file instead of saving it. In that case, use the original website's download option.

Is ViralVault free?

Yes. This website tool is free to use. Your mobile data or internet provider may still charge for data usage.

Does ViralVault store my links?

This static page does not send your pasted URL to a ViralVault server. Your browser requests the media directly from the URL you enter. The source website may have its own logging and privacy practices.

Can I download copyrighted content?

Only download content when you own it or have permission, and comply with the source website's rules and applicable law.

Privacy policy

ViralVault is a static webpage. The URL you enter is used by your browser to request the media from the source address. This page does not provide an account system or upload your link to a ViralVault backend. The source website may collect technical data under its own privacy policy. If analytics or advertising services are added later, this policy should be updated to explain them.
