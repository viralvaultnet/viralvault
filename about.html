About ViralVault

A small website with a simple goal: clear, useful information for everyday digital tasks.

ViralVault is a digital resource website. We build free online tools, such as our word and character counter, and publish beginner-friendly guides about images, videos and getting started online.

What we believe

  • Helpful first: every page should solve a real problem for the reader.
  • Original and honest: we write our own guides and do not make unrealistic promises.
  • Respectful of copyright: we always encourage using content you own or have permission to use.
  • Private by design: our tools run in your browser wherever possible.
