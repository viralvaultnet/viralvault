Video Download Guide

Learn about video formats and file sizes, and the safe, permitted ways to access downloadable media.

Common video formats

  • MP4: the most widely supported format. It plays on almost every phone, computer and TV.
  • WebM: an open format made for the web, often smaller files.
  • MOV: common on Apple devices, usually larger files.
  • MKV: flexible container often used for high-quality movies, but not supported everywhere.

Understanding file size

File size depends on length, resolution and quality. As a rough idea, one minute of 1080p video is often around 50 to 150 MB, while 720p is noticeably smaller. If your storage or data is limited, choose a lower resolution.

Safe ways to get a video

  1. Use the platform's built-in download or offline option when it exists, for example in the official app.
  2. Download content you own, such as videos you uploaded yourself, from your account settings.
  3. Look for videos published under licenses that allow downloading, such as Creative Commons or royalty-free stock libraries.
  4. Ask the creator for permission if you want to reuse their work.

Stay safe online

  • Be careful with websites full of pop-ups or fake "Download" buttons.
  • Do not install unknown programs just to save a video.
  • Never enter passwords or payment details on a site you do not trust.
Only save content you own or have permission to save. Downloading some videos may break a platform's terms or copyright law.
